# Apply the L∞P brand

## Read the source of truth

Read docs/design-system.md and docs/static-scrollytelling-mode.md before changing
Journey expression. Treat FIELD LOOP and 現場を、仕組みから変える。 as the
current direction, not permission to invent final claims.

Use only owned or approved assets. Keep missing imagery, case studies, customer
proof, metrics, legal facts, and final copy explicit. Never copy source, CSS,
wording, or media from the tea-leaf reference.

## Shape the visual language

Make the work quiet, premium, human-centered, intelligent, future-oriented,
real-world, architectural, and restrained. Build hierarchy with Japanese
typography, proportion, whitespace, material contrast, and a few precise
blue/cyan accents.

The Journey is still-first. Preserve each approved scene image and use code
layers to clarify its chapter:

- FIELD: minimal atmosphere, almost no structure;
- ORDER: sparse paths, alignment, and nodes;
- DESIGN: grid, planes, frames, and measured geometry;
- BUILD: organized route illumination and restrained activation;
- ADOPT: lower graphic intensity and human/environmental emphasis;
- RETURN: convergence, circulation, and the only infinity.

Overlays are abstract. Do not present them as real telemetry or measured results.

## Keep copy honest

Keep Journey copy in configuration/content, outside rendering and Timeline code.
Use factual development identity such as 01 / FIELD while approval is pending.
Do not bake essential copy into stills or overlays. Check Japanese wrapping and
do not force English line-breaking conventions onto it.

## Protect the RETURN infinity

An infinity may appear only in RETURN. Require one perfectly horizontal
interwoven ribbon, two fully closed uncropped elliptical loops, a natural
front/back crossing, and restrained blue-to-cyan optical light.

Reject a center bar, ring, sphere, capsule, mechanical core, broken/cropped loop,
vertical symbol, earlier-scene infinity, and cyberpunk bloom. If a compliant
formation is not ready, use a quiet circulation state or empty integration slot.
Do not ship a bad infinity.

## Reject

Reject generic SaaS dashboards/card walls, stock or fake production imagery,
cheap gradients, neon overload, random glowing blobs, excessive glassmorphism,
fake AI decoration, unsupported claims, and motion that competes with scene
meaning.

## Handoff

State which stills, crops, copy, and RETURN polish remain unapproved. Check dark
and light surfaces, Desktop/Tablet/Compact/short landscape, reduced motion,
keyboard, focus, and contrast. Do not claim visual PASS without browser evidence.
